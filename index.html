Cash, stocks and credit
in one account.

01 · BIOMETRIC SIGN-UP

Sign up in seconds.

Create your account with a biometric scan. There’s no seed phrase and no app to install.

02 · TOP UP

Top up with the coin you hold.

Send USDC, USDT, USDe, USDS, DAI or EURC on Base to your account. They arrive as cash, ready to earn, spend or invest.

03 · CASH THAT EARNS

Earn on the cash you keep.

It grows in savings by about 4.0% a year, at a variable rate. You still see one balance, and you can spend or invest from it anytime without withdrawing first.

04 · SEVEN STOCKS

Buy stocks, Apple to Tesla.

Trade the seven Coinbase tokenized stocks: Apple, Amazon, Alphabet, Meta, Microsoft, NVIDIA and Tesla. They track the share price, but they’re not company shares.

05 · BUY IN SECONDS

Type an amount and confirm.

You see what you get before you sign. The buy arrives in seconds, and you sell it back the same way.

06 · CREDIT BUY

Buy more, backed by what you hold.

Your stocks can back USDC credit, so you buy more without having to sell anything. Pick which stocks back the buy and see the limit before you sign. Only what the buy needs is used as backing.

07 · REPAY & FREE UP

Repay and free up in one go.

See what you owe, what it costs a month and what’s still available. Repay from your cash and free up the stocks behind it, all in one signature.

08 · CREDIT HEALTH

Know how safe your credit is.

Your credit health shows how far your stocks can fall before any are sold. It reads Safe from 1.50, Watch down to 1.10 and Act below that.

09 · PROTECT

Let Protect guard your credit.

If your credit health falls under 1.03, Protect repays from your cash and brings it back to 1.12. If cash isn’t enough, it sells only stock that backs your credit, and never on a weekend.

10 · REBALANCE

Use Rebalance to stay on target.

Pick your mix, and Rebalance trades back toward it every market day. It never touches your cash or the stock backing your credit.

11 · LEADERBOARD

Keep track of the greediest.

Everyone joins with their first move. The board ranks your return on your own money since you joined, so winning with credit moves you up.

Before any call, JAW’s manager checks
  1. The right spender. Only GooseExecutor can use it.
  2. Still active. You haven’t turned it off.
  3. Inside its dates. It has started and hasn’t ended.
  4. Every call is on the list. One call off the list and nothing runs.
After the calls run
  1. Approvals go back to zero. JAW’s manager resets every approval the move made.
  2. GooseExecutor checks your pockets. If money would end up anywhere but your own account, the whole move is undone.
Every 10 minutes

See how each round runs.

  1. 01GooseExecutor wakes up.A runner with no money or permission wakes it up.
  2. 02Each account gets a plan.Protect always goes first, and one account failing never stops the others.
  3. 03JAW checks, then runs.Only moves your permission allows get through.
  4. 04GooseExecutor rechecks.Money stays in your own pockets, or the move is undone.
